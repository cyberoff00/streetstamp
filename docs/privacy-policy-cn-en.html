Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 19, 2026

Worldo ("we", "our", or "the App") is a journey tracking and city discovery application. This policy applies to the account-free edition of Worldo. We know how sensitive location data is, and this edition is built around one principle: your data belongs to you.

This edition of Worldo has no account system and we operate no servers. Your routes, photos, and memories are stored only on your device, and — optionally — in your own iCloud account for syncing between your own devices. We cannot access or read any of it.

1. Information We Process

Content you create

  • Journey content: Route tracks, GPS coordinates, timestamps, and the photos, notes, and mood entries you add. Stored only on your device and in your personal iCloud space.
  • Avatar customization: Your traveler avatar's appearance and gear. Stored locally only.

Data processed to make features work

  • Precise location (GPS): Collected only after you start recording a journey or explicitly grant "Always Allow" background permission, and used to draw your routes. Location data stays on your device — we have no servers to send it to.
  • Motion & fitness data: Walking/running/cycling/transit detection from Core Motion, used to improve recording accuracy and power Lifelog.
  • Health data (HealthKit): Daily step counts are read for display only after you grant permission; workout sessions may be written to keep background recording stable. Health data never leaves your device and is never used for advertising.
  • Camera and photo library: Accessed only when you actively add a photo. We never touch photos you did not pick.
  • Device and diagnostic information: App version, OS version, device model, language, and anonymized crash diagnostics.

This edition does not collect: phone number, email, passwords, contacts, social graphs, clipboard contents, or advertising identifiers (IMEI/IDFA).

2. How Information Is Used

  • Recording, displaying, and replaying your journeys and city footprints
  • Showing daily steps, movement history, and moods in Lifelog
  • Syncing data between your own devices via your personal iCloud (Apple CloudKit)
  • Improving tracking accuracy with motion activity data
  • Processing subscription purchases through the Apple App Store
  • Diagnosing crashes and stability issues

We never use your personal information for advertising or profiling, and we never sell or rent it to anyone.

4. Data Storage and Security

  • Your journeys, photos, and Lifelog data are stored locally on your device by default.
  • If you enable iCloud sync, data is stored in your own Apple account's private iCloud container, accessible only to you.
  • We operate no servers of our own; your personal data is never uploaded to us.
  • All network traffic (map tiles, geocoding, weather) uses HTTPS/TLS encryption.

5. Data Sharing

We do not share your personal information with third parties, except:

  • Map and geocoding services: Coordinates are sent to the map providers above to render maps and resolve city names.
  • Weather service: To show weather effects over your tracks, your current coordinates are sent to the Open-Meteo weather API. No identity information is required by that service.
  • Legal requirements: Where disclosure is required by applicable law or valid legal process.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Your data stays on your device until you delete it in the App or uninstall the App.
  • You can delete any journey, photo, or Lifelog entry in the App at any time.
  • iCloud data can be removed via the App's settings or iOS Settings → Apple Account → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.
  • Uninstalling the App removes all local data.

7. Your Rights

  • You can revoke location, motion, health, camera, photo, or notification permissions in iOS Settings at any time.
  • You can review, correct, and delete all of your data inside the App — it is always under your control.
  • Because this edition has no accounts and collects no identity data, there is no account to delete and nothing to request from us; deletion is fully in your hands.
  • For questions or complaints about data handling, contact us at the email below.

If you are in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland: since your personal data never reaches us, the rights of access, rectification, portability, and erasure under GDPR are exercised directly on your device as described above. If you are a California resident: we do not sell or share personal information as defined by the CCPA/CPRA.

8. Children's Privacy

Worldo is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly process personal information of children under that age.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Significant changes will be communicated in the App or by updating the date above. Continued use after changes take effect constitutes acceptance.
